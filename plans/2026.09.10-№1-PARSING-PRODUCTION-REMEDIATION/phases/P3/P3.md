# P3 — Recovery, storage и timeouts

> **Phase Assurance | v1.**
> Impact: internal-integration
> Integration Seams: document.storage · pipeline.recovery · horizon.timeouts
> Live or Irreversible: no
> Audit Intent: auto
> Rationale: Item-level fault regressions and targeted reviews must cover storage/retry/recovery seams. A phase audit is not default: it may be proposed only for a named residual cross-item risk and explicit owner consent.

## Phase Context

> **Контракт фазы (contract-first).** Потребляет: terminal P2 trusted lifecycle · Производит:
> гарантированный download retry, truthful source/representation storage, автоматическую сверку
> потерянных continuations и согласованные worker budgets · Границы: внешний live pilot и итоговый
> verdict принадлежат P4; новый workflow engine/outbox не вводится без доказанного gap и D#.

Scope Included: A5, A6, NEW-2, A4, A7; one retry owner; DB→queue fault windows; false/exception
storage writes; same-hash healing; real-worker timeout/kill validation. Scope Excluded: LLM quality,
capacity SLA и платные production calls. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/00-source-synthesis.md`,
A4–A7/NEW-2).

## Sequence and dependencies

P3.1 → P3.2 → P3.3 создают единый storage/retry contract; P3.4 замыкает cross-stage recovery;
P3.5 согласует budgets и доказывает поведение настоящим worker.

## DoR (Definition of Ready)

- [x] Direct `handle()` не считается queue-semantics evidence.
- [x] False/exception/crash windows перечислены по source и prepared objects.
- [x] Один retry owner и atomic claim обязательны.
- [x] Timeout matrix включает next-call reserve, retry_after, lease и shutdown grace.

## Phase Handoff

После P3.5: direct-close фазы и затем независимый P4.1. Audit требует отдельного доказанного residual risk и адресного owner consent.
