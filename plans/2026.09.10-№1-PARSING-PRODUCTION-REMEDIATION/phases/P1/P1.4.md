### P1.4 — Исключить concurrent execution одного backlog/delta run

**Intent:** Исправить A9 scheduler guard и durable owner-token lease на весь processor run, включая manual/multi-host launches и crash expiry. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `PARSING_PRODUCTION_ACCEPTANCE.md:239-257`).

**Why:** `startOrResume()` возвращает одну Running row тому же owner, но короткая transaction не сериализует два scanner process; concurrent cursor updates могут теряться. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `findings/00-source-synthesis.md`, A9).

**Scope Included:** `withoutOverlapping`/named scheduler locks for backlog and delta; DB-backed execution owner token+expiry/heartbeat; atomic acquire/renew/release; no-op/busy outcome for second executor; stale lease takeover that resumes cursor; real two-connection/process regressions for backlog and delta.

**Scope Excluded:** one-running-per-crawler identity policy; Redis-only lock as sole protection; unbounded lock without expiry; fresh job uniqueness redesign.

**Implementation Rules:** Scheduler lock uses shared production cache and is defense-in-depth; correctness lives in atomic DB lease bound to run+owner token. Only token owner may update cursor/complete/release. Lease duration exceeds one safe slice, renews with heartbeat, and expires after crash; takeover rereads durable cursor. Lock ordering is fixed and transaction-scoped. RAG:— (repo-grounded: `research/01-remediation-architecture.md`).

**Code Guidance:** Use two independent PostgreSQL connections/processes, not mocks. Worker 1 must hold lease while worker 2 attempts entry; assert worker 2 never calls scanner and does not mutate cursor/stats. Kill/omit release, advance beyond expiry, then worker 2 acquires and resumes exact checkpoint. Test both backlog subject key and delta owner. Do not solve only with `withoutOverlapping`; manual launch must remain protected. No broad `Cache::lock` fallback if DB lease fails.
