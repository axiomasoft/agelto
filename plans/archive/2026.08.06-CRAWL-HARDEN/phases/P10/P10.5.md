### P10.5 — Разделить parse health и business delta листинга

**Intent:** Healthcheck должен считать healthy неизменившийся распарсенный блок и broken реальную
потерю разбора.

**Why:** `rows_new + rows_updated` отвечает на вопрос «изменились ли данные», а ListingShapeCheck
использует его как «распарсились ли строки» (`RAG:— (repo-grounded: F7 audit, D136)`).

**Scope Included:** (1) baseline `prc_fetches.rows_parsed`/`rows_errors`, model/factory/logger;
(2) `CsvBlockIngestor` counts accepted parsed rows and per-row mapping/ingest errors without hiding
them; (3) `FetchResult`/fetch write carries both metrics; (4) ListingShapeCheck and baseline use the
new signal; (5) unchanged-success, all-errors and partial-errors tests; (6) operator/runbook labels
name parse failure, not empty ingest.

**Scope Excluded:** changing new/updated semantics · treating ignored/deduplicated source rows as
mapping errors without an explicit category · disabling row-level resilience · calibrating a new
external EIS threshold from invented data · altering non-200 signal.

**Implementation Rules:** 1) Define and test the accounting identity for every source row:
parsed, ignored/deduplicated and error categories must explain `rows_total`; do not silently drop a
class. 2) `rows_parsed` counts successfully mapped/accepted rows even when procurement is unchanged;
`rows_errors` counts caught row failures. 3) Null metrics on historical fetches are `unknown`, not
zero; health window handles them explicitly. 4) Non-empty + all parsed + zero business delta is not
bad. 5) Non-empty + zero parsed or error share beyond an explicit existing/new named threshold is
bad; chosen threshold and denominator are pinned in baseline and tests. 6) Baseline migration is
edited in place; no prod follow-up migration. 7) Log/error samples must not expose raw sensitive
payload beyond current policy.

**Code Guidance:** Keep parse counters in the ingest result returned to `FetchLogger`; do not make
healthcheck reparse artifacts. Name baseline keys after the measured property. Запрещённые
shortcuts: declaring every `rows_new=0` healthy; using `rows_total-rows_new-rows_updated` as parsed;
coalescing historical null to zero; weakening the check threshold until current fixtures pass.
Анти-самообман: a factory-created fetch is insufficient; at least one test must pass a real
unchanged CSV row through `CsvBlockIngestor`. Анти-подгонка: an all-errors fixture must stay
Broken even if other window samples are healthy at the named threshold.

**Validation:** `migrate:fresh --seed` green and columns non-null for new logged fetches · focused
`CsvBlockIngestor|ListingShapeCheck` tests green · unchanged real ingest returns parsed>0,
new=updated=errors=0 and health not Broken · all mapping errors returns parsed=0/errors>0 and
health Broken · baseline JSON parses · Pint/PHPStan · no new full-suite delta.

**Deliverables:** parse/error telemetry carriers · truthful ListingShapeCheck/baseline · updated
runbook · real unchanged/error regressions · application/test/docs item commit plus separate
bookkeeping commit.
