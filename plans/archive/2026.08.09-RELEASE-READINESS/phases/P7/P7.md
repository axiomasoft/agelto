1. Reconcile terminal P0/P6 evidence and freeze semantic case/tag/cardinality/response matrix.
2. Typed enums, versioned nested Analysis.result envelope, deterministic backfill and JSON indexes.
3. Spatie Data/provider schema/prompt/eval/gold contract with structural consistency validation.
4. Backend-owned typed disposition/reason, idempotent short mutation and after-commit extraction.
5. Authorized Analysis Filament fields, tags anchors, filters/search and readable non-estimate result.
6. Format/unknown/non-estimate/single/multiple/rerun matrix, docs and extension architecture gates.

- Full estimate passport/row/totals extraction, new non-estimate extractor or generic result table.
- Reusing `Document.kind` as semantic type or copying screening facts onto `prc_documents`.
- Relaxing P6 unsupported/unsafe outcomes, first-page screening budget or provider safety rules.
- Global Filament navigation redesign P9, general security audit P10, CI topology P11 and rehearsal P12.
