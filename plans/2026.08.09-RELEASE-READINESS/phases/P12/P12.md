deviation, waiver, Pending Work либо owner gate; local RED repair-forward исправляется в текущем
item до GREEN, после чего root продолжает цепь.

## Scope Included

1. Свежий official-source RAG и трассировка его фактов к конкретным operator carriers.
2. Safe host/repository/ingress/TLS/backup/deploy/release/rotation/rollback/restore scripts и runbook'и.
3. Closed production-profile/templates и hostile local fixtures без реальных secret values.
4. Source-derived human/agent command matrix, troubleshooting и deterministic drift checks.
5. Redacted evidence schemas, aggregate preparation verdict и brief следующего rehearsal plan.

## Scope Excluded

- Dedicated server, DNS change, GitHub deploy-key enrollment, registry/CI access и TLS issuance.
- Production deploy, scheduled/off-host backup, alert delivery, secret rotation, rollback или restore.
- Fresh-VM clean-room rehearsal, live browser/API proof и D45 production `READY|NOT_READY`.
- Fabricated external receipts, timestamps, hashes, policy values или runtime claims.
