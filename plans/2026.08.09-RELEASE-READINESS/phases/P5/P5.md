## Scope Included

1. Reconcile terminal P0 proxy/settings findings and freeze package/storage/precedence contract.
2. Dedicated typed settings repository with nullable existing-Proxy ID and Horizon-fresh reads.
3. Atomic migration from bool to profile/default/direct, centralized resolver semantics and typed source.
4. Secret-safe authorized Filament settings/profile forms with Russian explanatory UX.
5. Listing/detail/document/OKPD2 precedence, health, no-proxy, LLM and Playwright separation proof.

## Scope Excluded

- Raw proxy URL/credentials in settings, a second proxy inventory, pool selector or health table.
- Availability fallback from a configured profile/default to another source; silent direct on outage.
- TLS trust changes, LLM provider policy, Playwright MCP launcher behavior or proxy vendor rotation.
- Full Filament navigation/localization P9, cross-cutting quality audit P10, CI P11 and production rehearsal P12.
