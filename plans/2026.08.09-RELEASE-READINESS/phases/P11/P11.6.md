**Scope Included:** Terminal P11.5 · recompute post-P11.4 five-run serial/profile/coverage and selected-mode baselines · freeze runner/runtime/commands and D42 budgets · global/critical-file coverage gate with higher measured floor · three fixed random-order serial seeds · three selected parallel/shard repetitions with exact name/status parity · deliberate TIA acceptance rejection, guard/cache/private-key/security/build negative checks · regenerate shard timings · deterministic local aggregate `test:release` carrier · real PR/main/manual Actions run at exact candidate SHA with required jobs/artifacts · final risk/flake/performance/coverage report · testing/CI/P12 docs contract.

**Implementation Rules:** P11.5 terminal and risk matrix has zero critical/high gaps. Performance inputs are five clean no-TIA runs on one recorded fingerprint after test changes; hard budget for each metric is `max(median × 1.50, median + 5s)`, never lower than the measured median. Coverage floor is max(D42 70% global/55% per critical file, post-P11.4 measured floor) and cannot be softened. Random seeds are fixed/published; every run compares exact name/status sets and forbids retry-as-green. A runner fingerprint mismatch yields red `baseline_pending`, not silent skip. Budget refresh is a separate reviewable diff with no product mutation. Live Actions URL/SHA/artifact hashes are mandatory; unavailable push/run leaves item blocked with exact resume command. RAG:— (repo-grounded: D42 internal release policy).
**Code Guidance:** Gate scripts consume machine-readable JUnit/Clover/JSON, fail closed on missing/malformed/empty inputs and print actual/threshold/fingerprint. Keep generated raw artifacts out of Git; commit only budgets, schemas and summarized anchored evidence. Anti-self-deception: never raise a budget in the same product diff that exceeds it, rerun until a flake disappears, treat replay/cache hit as executed, or average away a red seed. Any failing critical row or guard is fixed at root with a regression test, not excluded.
**Validation:** `bash docker/tests/release-quality-contract.sh` — новый item-owned carrier обязан проверить terminal P11.5, zero critical/high gaps, reproducible five-run fingerprint/budgets, global/critical coverage floors, three deterministic random-order set-parity runs, repeated selected D40 mode, release rejection of TIA, negative guard/cache/key/security/build fixtures, no-soft-point P10+full+coverage+E2E+build/security target, exact-SHA live CI evidence and secret-clean artifacts/logs, anchored report plus docs/Task/citation/git gates.
**Deliverables:** Committed non-regressing coverage/performance/determinism contracts and scripts, zero-flake repeated evidence, real candidate-SHA CI proof, updated shard/baseline artifacts, final P11 quality report/P12 consumer contract and separate final P11 commit.
