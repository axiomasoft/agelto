# P8.6 — full-suite blocker outside the declared Files contour

`bash docker/tests/platform-extension-contract.sh` reached the full
`php artisan test --no-tia` gate after the P8.6 factory, focused extension, migration,
Filament, config-cache, Pint and scoped PHPStan gates. It stopped at
`tests/Unit/Architecture/DomainLayoutTest.php` because the root-legal registry does not
contain `App\Enums\Procurement\StatusMappingApplicability`.

The enum was introduced by P8.3 in commit `44168b91` but neither that item nor P8.6 lists
`tests/Unit/Architecture/DomainLayoutTest.php` in `Files`. The failure is deterministic:

```text
App\Enums\Procurement\StatusMappingApplicability.php лежит в корне слоя
App\Enums\Procurement, но не входит в реестр root-legal классов.
```

P8.6 must not silently expand into the P3.4 domain-layout guard. The minimal repair is one
allowlist entry for `StatusMappingApplicability` in that test, followed by the same full
no-TIA carrier. Owner authorization is required to add this exact path to the P8.6 scope.
