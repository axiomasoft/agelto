# P3.5 document cutover: assertion and contract dispositions

Executed-case accounting proves that named targets ran; it does not establish semantic equivalence.
The previous N-to-one `handler_not_activated` mappings are superseded. FixtureRun has six distinct
cases with working-copy/hash/link/platform, auto/manual provenance, active-run rejection, screening
gate, both-step supersede/reset, and shared importer identity assertions. Ordinary dispatch is
replaced by ready Process work/run assertions; paid AI is not activated.

`tests/Fixtures/ProcessCore/Documents/p35-semantic-dispositions.json` records the reviewable scope.
Rows marked CHANGED CONTRACT do not claim the old behavior is equivalent: nullable disk fallback,
mutable representations, implicit direct egress, legacy lease/status/queue ownership and unconditional
staging deletion were replaced by explicit canonical/Process safety contracts. Format/hostile archive
assertions remain in the retained corpus. Full provider execution and load are outside P3.5.

The original AI recovery, orphan-run, retry-at and manual-rerun assertions are also retained
through a guarded frozen historical harness. Those cases are compatibility evidence for unmigrated
fake AI, not proof of new runtime feeder/provider activation. The app architecture guard rejects
any import of that historical namespace. Exact source/harness hashes are recorded in the JSON.
